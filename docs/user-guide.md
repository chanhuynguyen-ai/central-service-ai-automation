# User guide

## Employee

1. Sign in with an employee account.
2. Select **New request**.
3. Enter a specific title and describe the affected service, business impact, desired
   outcome, and deadline.
4. Select a category and priority when known. AI triage records its recommendation separately.
5. Submit and retain the `CSR-` reference.
6. Use **Requests** to follow status and approval updates.

Do not include passwords, private keys, medical information, or unnecessary personal data.

## Approver

1. Open **Approvals** and review the original request before the AI summary.
2. Check requester, business justification, requested access or cost, and policy.
3. Approve or reject with a concise reason.
4. For uncertain or high-risk cases, return the request for clarification outside the demo flow.

The AI recommendation does not replace the approver's judgment.

## Operations administrator

1. Use **Overview** and **Analytics** for backlog and SLA review.
2. Use **Automation** to inspect workflow status, provider, latency, and errors.
3. Investigate repeated fallbacks or category corrections.
4. Review audit events before changing a request lifecycle state.

## Policy assistant

Ask one focused question at a time. For request-specific answers, include a `CSR-` reference.
Check the source title and version below the answer. If the assistant reports insufficient
context, contact the service owner instead of treating a general answer as policy.
